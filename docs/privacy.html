Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

Short version: we never see your chats, never ask for passwords, and your answers stay on your device.

What Stays on Your Device

Your answers to the four questions (who's at home, what fills your week, what you want help with, how you like answers) and which AI you picked are saved in your own browser so your cheat sheet stays the same when you come back. They are never sent to us. You can clear them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your Chats

When you tap a shortcut, your AI opens in a new tab and your conversation happens between you and that AI company. We can't see it. Those companies have their own privacy policies.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details and email address; we receive your email address and the payment amount so we can send help or a refund. We never see your full card number.

Visit Counts

We count visits and a few taps (trying a free shortcut, starting and finishing the four questions, tapping a shortcut on your sheet, tapping the payment button, opening your paid sheet, printing it, copying your "about me" note, tapping "Send it" to share the site) with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service that doesn't use cookies. Each count can include which ad or link brought you and, for the questions, how many help topics you picked. It never includes which shortcut you tapped, your answers, which AI you use, your name, your email or anything you type. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, we don't count your visit.

Emails You Send Us

If you email us, we keep your message to answer it and handle refunds.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We don't ask for, store or use passwords to any AI service.
  • We don't use cookies for tracking.
