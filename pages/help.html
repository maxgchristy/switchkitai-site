What is Kitchen Table?

A list of one-tap shortcuts for everyday jobs: planning dinners, writing emails, sorting out bills, planning trips, getting help with your phone and more. Tap a line and your AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Copilot) opens with the words already written. You don't need to know what to type.

Do I need to pay for an AI?

No. Every shortcut works with the free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. Some of them ask you to sign in or create a free account.

Couldn't I just type these myself?

Yes. Each shortcut is just well-chosen words for your AI, and you're welcome to write your own. The cheat sheet saves you working out what to ask and how to ask it.

I tapped a line and nothing was typed in. What now?

If you're inside the Facebook or Instagram app, tap the three dots and choose "Open in browser" for the smoothest experience.

Yes. AI is helpful but not always right. Double-check anything important, especially about money, health or legal matters, with a qualified person.
